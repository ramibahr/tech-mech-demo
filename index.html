What We Do

Our Services

A full range of automotive services using quality parts and expert technicians. Transparent pricing — no hidden surprises, ever.

General Vehicle Maintenance

Oil changes, filters, fluid top-ups, belts and routine check-ups to keep your car in peak condition and prevent costly breakdowns.

Engine Diagnostics & Repair

State-of-the-art diagnostic tools covering all makes and models. Warning light on? We get to the root cause and fix it right the first time.

Brake Services

Full brake system inspection, pad and disc replacement, caliper servicing and brake fluid changes. Safety-critical — never compromise.

Suspension & Steering

Shock absorbers, springs, control arms, wheel alignment and steering rack repairs. Restore handling, comfort and tyre life.

Transmission Services

Gearbox diagnostics, clutch replacement, automatic and manual transmission servicing to ensure smooth power delivery and longevity.

Electrical System Repair

Battery testing and replacement, alternator and starter diagnosis, wiring faults, sensor replacement and full vehicle electrical checks.

Bodywork

Dent removal, scratch repair, panel work and minor body restoration. We'll have your vehicle looking as good as it drives.

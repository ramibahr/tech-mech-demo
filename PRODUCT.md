# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Local vehicle owners searching for a trustworthy nearby garage, arriving to evaluate the business and book a repair or maintenance visit (routine servicing, diagnostics, brakes, etc.) — not fleet managers or dealerships.

## Product Purpose

Tech-Mech's marketing/booking site for a real, operating automotive repair and maintenance garage. It exists to build trust with prospective customers and convert them into a booked visit via the contact/booking form.

## Positioning

Automotive repair with full transparency: no hidden costs, no unnecessary upselling, clear explanation of work before it starts, competitive/fair pricing, quality parts. Services cover all makes and models.

## Operating Context

- Booking currently happens through an on-page contact/message form; visitors expect a prompt follow-up to confirm the appointment.
- Opening hours are displayed in the header announcement bar and footer (Mon–Fri, Sat, closed Sun) — worth reconciling exact hours against the real business hours before shipping.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Booking system (firm requirement):** SimplyBook.me is the intended online scheduling backend. The current form has a note that SimplyBook integration is pending — this is a planned build item, not an open decision.
- Services offered (from current copy): general vehicle maintenance, engine diagnostics & repair, brake services, suspension & steering, transmission services, electrical system repair, bodywork.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Tech-Mech.
- Voice: direct, trust-first, no-upsell — "transparent pricing, experienced mechanics."

## Evidence on Hand

This is a real business, but the current site content was built with placeholder details that must be replaced with real information before launch — future work must not treat these as confirmed facts or invent replacements without asking:

- Contact details are placeholders: phone `+44 7XXX XXX XXX`, email `hello@techmech.co.uk`, address `123 Garage Lane, Your Town, AB1 2CD`.
- Stats in the hero ("12+ Years Experience", "4,000+ Vehicles Serviced", "5★ Google Rating") are unconfirmed — need real figures from the business.
- The three testimonials (initials JD, SM, RK) are placeholder reviews, not real customer quotes.

## Product Principles

1. Transparency over persuasion — always explain the "why" behind pricing and recommended work.
2. Never fabricate business facts (stats, reviews, hours, contact info) — flag placeholders instead of inventing real-sounding data.
3. Booking conversion is the primary success metric; keep the path from landing to booked visit short and low-friction.
4. Serve all makes and models — don't design copy or imagery that implies a narrow vehicle specialization.
